# Changelog

## 0.4.1 – 2026-11-05

- System pages: styles for the link request of kopexa/mautic-preferences-center
  (`/email/preferences`: email field, button, error).

## 0.4.0 – 2026-11-05

- Footer links in German with their own text: „Abmelden oder Themen wählen“ ({unsubscribe_url}) and „Im Browser ansehen“ ({webview_url}). Mautic's {unsubscribe_text} / {webview_text} render English default texts unless configured; the QA now rejects them.
- System pages (html/message.html.twig) in the Kopexa layout: unsubscribed, re-subscribed and Mautic's built-in preference form; a preference center page (theme kopexa-preferences) is passed through with Mautic's form scripts. Shared with all Kopexa themes.
- assets/fonts/kopexa-fonts.css: the brand fonts as a self-hosted stylesheet (used as the builders' editor font instead of Google Fonts).

## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-03

- Block library for assistants (`mcp/blocks.json`, generated by `qa/blocks.mjs`): panels *hero*, *article* and *cards* with their elements, as MJML (editable in the builder) and as HTML compiled by MJML (for sending). kopexa/mautic-mcp-bundle builds brand emails from it (`create_email_from_layout`). The QA proves that assembled HTML is identical to MJML's own output.

## 0.2.0 – 2026-11-02

- Fix: header and footer turned white after an email was saved and reopened in the builder (logo and claim invisible). The builder gives sections without an `mj-class` its white surface token and drops inline attributes equal to MJML's defaults. Header and footer now use their own tokens (`k-navy`, `k-claim`, `k-fine`); `qa/check.mjs` enforces both builder rules.
- Shared tokens for the theme variants: `k-note`, `k-hero`, `k-hero-label`, `k-hero-h1`, `k-hero-lead`.
- New variants in their own packages: *Kopexa · Anmeldung bestätigen* (`kopexa-confirm`, double opt-in with `{doi_link}`) and *Kopexa · Hero + Split* (`kopexa-hero`).

## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-01

- Kopexa email theme for the GrapesJS MJML builder, with header, footer, buttons and typography tokens (`t-*` classes).
- Quality gate in `qa/` (not part of the package). It checks:
  - HTML size and links
  - contrast
  - email client compatibility
  - spam score
  - rendering screenshots
